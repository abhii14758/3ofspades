Task 6 — Player Position: Current vs. New

6-player game example. You are Seat 3. How should the table look to you?

❌ CURRENT — Relative View (You always at bottom)
YOU (Seat 3)
Seat 0 (Player A)
Seat 5
Seat 1
Seat 4
Seat 2

Seat 0 sees a different layout. Players disagree on who is "left" or "right".

✅ NEW — Absolute View (Fixed seats, You highlighted)
Seat 0 (Player A)
Seat 1
Seat 2
YOU (Seat 3)
Seat 4
Seat 5

Every player sees this exact layout. YOU can be anywhere — just highlighted in blue.

⚠️ Key consequence: If you joined as Seat 3, you'll see your own cards at the top of the screen (not the bottom). This is different from most card games but matches a real physical table where everyone sees the same thing.
